Stew Recipe

Description

The following recipe has been tried be me on many occasions, and it's always a hit! It is hearty and delicious, and always brings warms into cold winter evenings.

Ingredients

  • 1. 2 pounds of beef stew meat, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 2. 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3. 1 teaspoon salt
  • 4. 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 5. 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 6. 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 7. 1/4 teaspoon onion powder
  • 8. 1/4 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 9. 1/4 teaspoon dried rosemary
  • 10. 1/4 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 11. 1/4 teaspoon dried basil
  • 12. 1/4 teaspoon dried parsley

Instructions

  1. 1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, salt, black pepper, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, thyme, rosemary, oregano, basil, and parsley.
  2. 2. Add the beef stew meat to the bowl and toss to coat the meat evenly with the flour and spice mixture.
  3. 3. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil over medium-high heat. Add the coated beef stew meat to the pot and brown on all sides, about 5-7 minutes. Remove the browned meat from the pot and set aside.
  4. 4. In the same pot, add 1 chopped onion and 2 minced garlic cloves. Sauté until the onion is translucent, about 3-4 minutes.
  5. 5. Add 4 cups of beef broth, 1 cup of red wine (optional), 2 chopped carrots, 2 chopped potatoes, and 1 cup of chopped celery to the pot. Stir to combine.
  6. 6. Return the browned beef stew meat to the pot. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to low. Cover and simmer for 1.5 to 2 hours, or until the meat is tender and the vegetables are cooked through.
  7. 7. Taste the stew and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper as needed. Serve hot and enjoy!
